# User Guide

This guide explains how to navigate and utilize the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform.

## Navigating the Dashboards
1. **Executive Overview**: High-level platform KPIs including overall revenue, active accounts, profit margins, and monthly sales forecast trends.
2. **Sales Hub**: Detailed analysis of monthly order volumes and interactive margin simulation calculators.
3. **Finance Metrics**: Tracking opex, cash balance, debt balance, and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR).
4. **Operations Log**: Throughput rates, on-time delivery rates, and incident logging across all regional warehouses.
5. **Customer KPIs**: Account segmentations, engagement index, and customer health monitoring.
6. **Forecast Center**: Run model simulation forecasts dynamically across Revenue, Demand, and Churn models, including local SHAP feature impact explanation graphs.
7. **Risk Analytics**: Assess Customer Attrition Risk, Operational Stockout Risk, or Financial Solvency Risk based on adjustable simulator variables.
8. **Recommendation Desk**: Machine learning generated prescriptive business recommendations complete with priority indicators, ROI estimates, and execution timeframes.
